Before you read on

Enterprise Search is Team and Enterprise plans only, and an Owner has to switch it on for the organisation first. On Free, Pro or Max it isn't available — you can skip this lesson without missing anything the others depend on.

The one-line version

Enterprise Search is Lesson 05 and Lesson 08 fused and handed to you pre-assembled. That narrowness is the feature — it isn't a general assistant with your tools bolted on, it's a research desk that does one thing properly.

Part 03

What to ask it

It earns its keep on questions that span several sources — the ones where the answer isn't in any single place, and finding it by hand would mean opening four tabs.

Getting up to speed

  • "What happened yesterday while I was out?"
  • "Summarise key updates across the business from the last week."
  • "What are the current blockers on the Platform project?"

Policy and process

  • "What is our company's remote work policy?"
  • "How do I submit an expense report?"
  • "What's the process for requesting time off?"

Research and analysis

  • "What are the main reasons customers cite for choosing competitors?"
  • "Summarise discussions about the Q4 product roadmap."
  • "Find information about our customer onboarding process."

Onboarding new people

  • "How does our authentication system work?"
  • "Who should I talk to about learning the billing system?"
  • "What tools does the engineering team use for deployment?"

Project tracking

  • "Find discussions and documents related to the marketing campaign."
  • "What were the key decisions from last week's leadership meetings?"
  • "Summarise team contributions to the Infrastructure initiative."

The technique worth knowing

  • For genuinely thorough research, don't ask it to boil the ocean in one go — ask it to search one source at a time, then synthesise the findings.
  • This is Anthropic's own guidance, and it's the same incremental habit from Lesson 03: one step per turn, so you can see what each one found.

The tell: if answering the question yourself would mean opening more than two tools, this is the thing to ask.

Part 04

The two-part setup

Setup is a relay, not a checklist: one person does the first half for everyone, then each person does the second half for themselves. Work out which half is yours before you start following steps.

Half one · an Owner

Done once, for everyone

  1. Click "Ask Your Org" in the left sidebar.
  2. Click "Set up for your org" — or "Disable" to turn the feature off entirely.
  3. Connect the organisation's tools. Two are required: a Documents connector (Google Drive, SharePoint) and a Chat connector (Slack, Microsoft Teams). Email is recommended but optional.
  4. Use "+ Add more" for any other tools the team needs.
  5. Name the project — whatever you type becomes "Ask [Name]" in everyone's sidebar.
  6. Add a description and click "Finish set up."
→ then →
Half two · everyone else

Done once, by you

  1. Click the project in your sidebar — it'll be starred once an Owner has finished.
  2. Follow the guided onboarding to connect the recommended services.
  3. Authenticate with each service you want searched — Slack, Google, Microsoft 365 and so on. This is with your account, which is what keeps the results scoped to you.
  4. Start asking.

More connectors, better answers. You can add more later from "Connect" in the project's Instructions section — you don't have to decide everything on day one.

The word that costs people a week

It has to be an Owner — not an admin

Course material usually says "admins (Owners)" as if the two are interchangeable. They aren't, and getting this wrong is the most common reason a rollout stalls.

  • Only an Owner or Primary Owner can complete the initial setup. An Admin cannot. A member cannot.
  • Nothing in the interface says so. The project is enabled by default and appears in everyone's sidebar — so members can see it, click it, and get nothing useful, with no explanation why.
  • Not sure if you're one? Open Settings → Organization. If you can see the admin panel, you're an Owner. If you can't, find whoever manages your Claude plan — that's the person who has to act.

Source: Anthropic Help Centre, "Use enterprise search", checked August 2026.

When it's there but doesn't work
  1. Check the plan. Team or Enterprise — nothing below.
  2. Ask an Owner to confirm the feature is enabled for the organisation.
  3. Refresh, or sign out and back in. Genuinely fixes a surprising share of cases.
  4. Check the specific connector is enabled at the organisation level, not just the feature.
  5. Confirm you have an active account with the service you're trying to search.
  6. Check your organisation's policies don't block third-party integrations.
  7. Make sure you finished the authentication flow — half-completed auth looks identical to no auth.
Part 05

That's a lot of data. Is it safe?

Short answer: yes — and the reason is worth understanding rather than taking on trust, because it's the same principle that governed connectors in Lesson 08.

Guarantee 01

Your permissions, unchanged

It only surfaces what you already have permission to see in the original tool. Locked out of a Slack channel? Nothing from it will appear. Searching doesn't grant access — it inherits it.

Guarantee 02

Your conversations stay private

What you ask it is yours. The org-wide part is the connected knowledge, not your queries.

Guarantee 03

Nothing is copied elsewhere

Your connected data isn't indexed or stored separately. Questions go to the source systems and the answer comes back — there's no second copy of your company sitting somewhere new.

Worth saying to a nervous colleague: Enterprise Search doesn't widen anyone's access by a single document. It only makes the access they already had searchable in one place.
