What is
Claude?

The whole lesson, drawn as a map. One idea sits at the centre: Claude isn't a place you type questions — it's a place you hand over situations.

Route 01

The four ideas

If you remember nothing else from this lesson, remember these four. Everything later in the course leans on them.

01

Helpful, harmless, honest

Claude is trained with an approach called Constitutional AI: a set of principles that steer it away from toxic or discriminatory output and away from helping with illegal or unethical activity. The goal is an assistant that aligns with human values and works transparently.

02

More than a chatbot

Summarising, searching, creative and collaborative writing, Q&A, coding — handled reliably and predictably. Think thinking partner, not answer machine: something you take complex problems to, not just simple questions.

03

Steerable and collaborative

You can direct personality, tone and behaviour. Customers report Claude is less likely to produce harmful output, easier to converse with, and more steerable — so you reach the output you wanted with less back-and-forth.

04

Available wherever you work

The apps are on every plan type — Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. Sign in and your conversations, projects, memory and preferences follow you across web, desktop and mobile.

Route 02

Search box, or Claude?

Six real requests. Three belong in a search box, three need Claude. Read each one and decide before you open the answer — guessing first is what makes it stick.

The rule that sorts all six

A search box takes a question.
Claude takes the situation.

The dividing line was never the topic — exchange rates, invoices and reviews can all be typed into a search box. What separates the two columns is whether the answer needs you in it: your draft, your last deck, your read on the customer.

Request 01

"What's the exchange rate from US dollars to euros today?"

Show the answer
Search box

One fact, one right answer, nothing of yours in it.

Trips most people Request 02

"What's the difference between a purchase order and an invoice?"

Show the answer
Search box

It sounds like work, so people file it under Claude. But it's a definition — settled, public, and identical no matter who asks. Hand over your actual invoice problem and it changes columns.

Request 03

"How many working days are there between March 3 and March 24?"

Show the answer
Search box

A calculation with a single correct result. No judgement required.

Request 04

"Rewrite my update below so the delay reads as a decision, not an apology."

Show the answer
Claude — and you

Your words, your situation, your judgement call about tone. Nothing to look up.

Trips most people Request 05

"Best structure for a quarterly business review presentation"

Show the answer
Claude — and you

Typed like a query, but it's a situation. A useful structure depends on your business, your audience and last quarter's deck — so give Claude those instead of the bare phrase.

Request 06

"I've pasted a customer's complaint thread below. Help me work out what they're actually asking for before I reply."

Show the answer
Claude — and you

Interpretation of messy, specific material that exists nowhere but in your inbox.

Route 03

What it's good at

Five areas where Claude both automates work and augments it. Notice that each one assumes you bring material to it.

Writing and content creation

Social posts, professional emails, long reports. Because Claude takes direction on tone and personality, you iterate on structure and clarity together until your voice comes through.

Research and analysis

Explore angles, compile findings, pull insight out of data. Upload documents and Claude reads them with you — a 200K-token context window is roughly 500 pages, and supported models on paid plans go higher still, so long material can stay in one conversation.

Coding

One of Claude's strongest areas. Write, debug and — often more useful — have code explained back to you, across many languages.

Problem-solving and reasoning

Maths, strategy, analysis. Claude can answer near-instantly or slow down and reason first — a capability called Thinking. When a problem deserves care, it works through the steps before answering.

Learning new things

Claude adapts to your pace. Learning mode goes further: instead of handing over answers, it guides your reasoning, which is how you build the thinking rather than just receive it.

Route 04

Where it lives

Claude is the intelligence. These are the doors into it — same assistant, different room, each suited to a different kind of work.

Claude.ai, desktop and mobile

How most people work with Claude: ask, brainstorm, create and edit documents, analyse. This course lives here

Claude Code

An agentic tool built for developers, though it handles all kinds of file work on your desktop — editing files directly, running commands, making commits.

@Claude in Slack

Chat from the AI assistant header in any channel, or tag @Claude in a thread. Connected, Claude can search your workspace's channels, DMs and shared files for the context a good answer needs.

Claude Design

A space for turning ideas into working interfaces. Describe what you want — or start from a sketch or screenshot — and get an interactive prototype you can refine and hand to your team.

Claude for Microsoft 365

A sidebar inside Excel, PowerPoint, Word and Outlook — analyse, draft and edit in the document that's already open, carrying context from one app to the next.
