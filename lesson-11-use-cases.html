Part 03

How to read one of these guides

Every page in the gallery is built the same way. Knowing the structure means you can skim to the part you need instead of reading top to bottom.

01
Describe the taskA complete prompt, written out and ready to copy. This is the fastest way to see what a well-specified request actually looks like in your domain.
02
Give Claude context — read this one firstEach guide lists its required context: the connector to link, the setting to switch on, the file to upload. Skip it and you'll get a weaker result and blame the prompt. Some also list optional context, like turning on extended thinking, which is where the extra quality hides.
03
What Claude createsA worked example of the output. Read it before you run anything — it tells you what success is supposed to look like, so you can tell whether yours fell short.
04
Follow-up promptsHow to test it, refine it, and combine it with other work. Lesson 03's incremental habit, written out for that specific task.
05
Tricks, tips and troubleshootingThe part most people skip and shouldn't. It's where the guide explains why the thing works, not just how to run it.
End of the course

Eleven lessons. Pick one guide and actually run it.

Reading about a capability and using it once are not the same kind of knowing. Look at what's on your desk this week, find its shape in Part 01, open the matching guide, and follow it once — required context included. The second one takes a quarter of the time, and by the third you'll stop needing the guide.
